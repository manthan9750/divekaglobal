About Divekaa Global

Global Reach, Indian Roots.

Our Story

Divekaa Global was born with a simple belief — traditional Indian jaggery deserves a modern identity. In a world dominated by refined sugar and artificial sweets, we wanted to bring back nature’s original sweetness.

That’s how Gud Bite was created — bite-sized jaggery cubes designed for modern lifestyles, without compromising on health, tradition, or taste.

Every cube is crafted with care, combining Ayurveda-inspired ingredients with innovative flavors for everyday wellness.

Founder Vision

“We didn’t want to create just another sweet product. We wanted to build a healthy habit for millions of people.”

The idea behind Gud Bite came from observing how people struggle to balance taste and health. Divekaa Global bridges that gap.

- Founder, Divekaa Global
